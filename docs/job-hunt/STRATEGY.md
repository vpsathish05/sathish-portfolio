### 📍 Visa & Work Authorization Reality

**For UK:**
- Skilled Worker Visa requires employer sponsorship
- AI Engineer roles qualify (RQF Level 6+)
- Salary threshold: £38,700+ (or going rate for role)
- Many UK companies sponsor for AI talent shortage

**For US:**
- H-1B visa lottery (annual, uncertain)
- Remote "Contractor" arrangements possible
- Some companies hire via EOR (Employer of Record)
- Focus: Companies with global hiring infrastructure

**Best Strategy:** Target companies that:
1. Explicitly mention visa sponsorship, OR
2. Hire internationally via EOR (Remote.com, Deel, Oyster), OR
3. Are open to contractor relationships

### 4.2 Salary Negotiation

#### Market Rates Reference
| Level | US Remote | UK (with sponsorship) |
|-------|-----------|----------------------|
| Mid-level (3-5 yr) | $140K-$200K | £70K-£100K |
| Senior (5+ yr) | $200K-$300K | £90K-£130K |
| Staff/Principal | $250K-$400K | £120K-£180K |

#### Negotiation Strategy
1. **Never give a number first** - Ask for their range
2. **Anchor high** - Start 15-20% above target
3. **Total comp matters** - Equity, bonus, benefits, WFH stipend
4. **Have alternatives** - Best leverage is competing offers
5. **Get it in writing** - Verbal promises mean nothing

#### Questions to Ask Before Accepting
- Is this fully remote or hybrid?
- Any geographic restrictions?
- Is pay location-adjusted?
- Visa sponsorship available? (if applicable)
- What's the on-call expectation?
- How is performance evaluated remotely?
