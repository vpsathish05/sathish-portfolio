# ResumeHeader
The top of the CV/résumé: title, name, contact line and personal statement.

- Consumer provides `title`, `name`, `location` (city and country only), `links` (email, LinkedIn, site/GitHub) and `statement` (4–6 lines: who you are, the value you bring, the role you want next).
- UK and US conventions: no photo, no date of birth, no marital status, nationality or full address. Wrap the whole résumé in `.sg-resume`.
