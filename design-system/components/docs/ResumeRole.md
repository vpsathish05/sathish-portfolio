# ResumeRole
One job on the résumé: role, organisation, dates and impact bullets.

- Consumer provides `role`, `org`, `location`, `period`, optional `summary` (one line of scope) and `bullets`.
- Bullet formula: **action verb + what you built + measurable result** ("Architected… cutting… by 38%"). 3–5 bullets for recent roles, 1–2 for older ones. Reverse-chronological.
- Results must be real and non-confidential; describe clients by industry, never by name.
