# CapabilityGrid
What you architect, in six cells: the 'expertise' answer a hiring manager or client scans for first.

- Consumer provides `items` — `{title, summary, points?, code?}`. Six cells is the sweet spot (3×2, the default). Use `columns: 2` for four items or `columns: 4` for eight; it collapses to 2 then 1 column on smaller screens.
- Title = a capability area in plain words ("RAG & knowledge"), summary = one sentence on the outcome, points = 2–4 concrete techniques.
- Cells sit on a hairline grid (`line` gaps) with a top `signal` rule on hover. No icons, no percentages, no skill bars.
